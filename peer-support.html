A few supportive notes from friends and short guidance quotes from clinicians (examples).

"If you're feeling low, try a simple 4-4-4 breathing: breathe in for 4, hold 4, out for 4. Repeat for a minute to calm your nervous system."

— Friend

"Grounding exercise: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste. Do it slowly and notice the body."

— Peer Support

"Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR): tense each muscle group for 5 seconds then release, moving from toes up to your face. It can reduce anxiety and tension."

— Clinician tip

"Try a short guided imagery: imagine a safe peaceful place, notice colors, sounds, textures. Spend 2–3 minutes there when stress spikes."

— Therapist suggestion
